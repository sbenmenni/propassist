About

A new generation of property management.

Mission

Make prices fair and give property managers tools that make the work easier.

Everyone should have access to fair and transparent pricing for property management, whatever their location or property size. We give property managers the technology and resources to streamline operations and improve the experience for owners and tenants.

Vision

Property management that is accessible and hassle-free for everyone across the US.

When managing a property is simple, more people can own and manage one with ease and confidence.

Founded by Samir Benmenni and Oleksiy Malytskyy.

Propassist is led by its CEO and CMO, who built it on their own experience managing properties remotely.
